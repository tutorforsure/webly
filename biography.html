Narendra Modi Biography

Early Life & Education

Born on September 17, 1950, in Vadnagar, Gujarat, Narendra Damodardas Modi came from a humble background. Son of Damodardas Mulchand Modi, a tea seller, Modi's early life was marked by determination and service to society.

Political Journey

Modi's political career began with the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) in his youth. He joined the BJP in 1987 and served as Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014, transforming the state into an economic powerhouse.

Prime Minister of India

Since becoming Prime Minister in 2014, Modi has led transformative initiatives including Digital India, Make in India, Swachh Bharat, and Atmanirbhar Bharat, positioning India as a global leader.
